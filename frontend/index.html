How it works
Getting started with DiveBuddy
1
Record your dive
Use an underwater camera or your phone in a waterproof case. Mount it to capture wide footage of the reef.
2
Upload after surfacing
Open DiveBuddy, start a new dive log, and upload your video. Our AI scans it frame by frame.
3
Get your species report
See every species spotted, abundance, rarity, and your points. Unidentified species are flagged.
4
Climb the leaderboard
Rare species earn more points. Compete globally, by location, or just with friends.
